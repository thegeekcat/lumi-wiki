# Welcome to Lumi Wiki

Lumi Wiki is the open-web home for polished, share-safe knowledge from Hye, Lumi, and The Shadow Geeks.

This V1 is intentionally modest but useful:

- a clean public landing page
- a searchable public note index
- note cards with tags, sections, and dates
- an Apple-inspired reading experience
- a strict privacy boundary around source material

The important rule: **private Obsidian notes do not get published automatically.** Only approved notes in `content/public/*.md` are part of the site.

## What belongs here

Use Lumi Wiki for material that is already safe to show publicly: polished explanations, operating principles, public project notes, learning maps, and documentation that does not reveal private context.

## What does not belong here

Raw vault notes, personal reflections, client-sensitive material, private plans, unfinished drafts, and anything copied from Hye's Obsidian workspace without explicit public approval.
