# Design Principles

Lumi Wiki should feel calm, precise, and product-like: Apple-inspired, not an Apple clone.

## Visual direction

- light canvas: `#f5f5f7`
- white cards and reading surfaces
- primary text: `#1d1d1f`
- restrained gray hierarchy for secondary text
- blue only for interaction: `#0071e3` and `#0066cc`
- large, confident system typography
- generous whitespace
- minimal borders and shadows

## Interaction direction

Search, navigation, and calls to action should be obvious without feeling loud.

Use pill controls for actions and filters. Keep motion subtle: small hover lifts, focus rings, and clear active states.

## Content direction

The interface should make public notes feel curated rather than dumped. A good card quickly answers:

- What is this note?
- Why might I open it?
- Which section does it belong to?
- Is it current enough to trust?

## Anti-goals

Avoid dark cyber aesthetics, heavy gradients, decorative clutter, novelty animations, and anything that makes the wiki feel less trustworthy.
